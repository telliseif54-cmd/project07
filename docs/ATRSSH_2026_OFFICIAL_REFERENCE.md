## 1. Purpose and policy orientation
The call concerns thematic research projects for 2026 in social and human sciences. The official announcement frames research as a means to understand and help solve major societal problems, and emphasizes updating research approaches in response to current social transformations and challenges.

The announcement explicitly highlights challenges including:
- digitalization;
- psychological and social disorders;
- heritage and its relationship to development;
- social issues such as family;
- work;
- migration;
- urbanization.

The call presents thematic research projects as a practical mechanism for addressing research challenges. It is directed to research entities such as laboratories, research units and research centers, and to national researchers and competencies, including researchers residing abroad. The announcement also encourages openness to social and economic partners.

## 2. Research orientation required by the call
The official announcement states that proposed projects should go beyond merely descriptive work. It calls for an **analytical or comparative** approach and positions the projects as mechanisms for proposing field-oriented scientific solutions responding to challenges in the socio-economic environment at different levels.

Operational interpretation for this repository:
- A strong proposal should not stop at describing a phenomenon.
- The project should formulate a clear research problem and use analysis and/or comparison.
- The work should connect research findings to practical solutions and relevant users/decision-makers.
- The proposal should show how the research responds to a real challenge in the social/economic environment.

The four bullets above are repository interpretation derived from the wording of the official call, not quoted requirements beyond the source text.

## 3. Selection criteria
The official announcement states:

### Team and duration
- Research team size: **3 to 6 researchers**.
- The project coordinator must hold a **professorial rank / appropriate professor-grade status** as stated in the Arabic announcement.
- Typical project duration: **2 years**.

### Selection bases
Selection is based on:
1. A multidisciplinary research team and demonstrated competence of the proposed researchers for the project.
2. Alignment of project content with the proposed themes/axes and the stated problems/issues.
3. Precision and clarity of the proposed research outputs.
4. Contribution/involvement of social partners from the relevant sectors.
5. Local institutional placement of approved projects within research entities.

## 4. Funding rules stated in the call
The official announcement states:
- Maximum funding: **2,000,000 DZD**.
- This amount is dedicated **exclusively to operating/management expenses** (`عملية التسيير`).
- Funding is divided into **two tranches**.
- The first tranche is around **50%**.
- The operating budget is paid into the affiliation institutions of approved project coordinators / host entities.
- Members receive an annual research allowance under **Article 14 of Executive Decree No. 21-144 dated 17 April 2021**, which regulates conditions for part-time scientific research and technological development activities and their compensation.

Important: this document records what the 2026 call page states. It does not independently interpret the legal decree or infer additional eligible/ineligible expenditure categories.

## 6. Live application form — verified structure
The official live form is titled **Appel à projets thématiques 2026** and is organized into **7 stages**:

1. Identification
2. Description
3. Planning
4. Team
5. Equipment
6. Needs
7. Dissemination

### Stage 1 — Identification du projet
Observed fields:
- Project title (`Intitulé du projet`)
- Research axis (`Axe de recherche`) — selectable field
- Research sub-axis (`Sous-axe de recherche`) — selectable field

### Stage 2 — Description du projet
Observed fields and visible limits:

- **Context and objectives** (`Contexte et objectifs`) — **1000 characters** shown.
  - The form asks for the project context, challenges/opportunities, specific objectives, and expected research/innovation impact.
- **Problem statement** (`Problématique`) — **1000 characters** shown.
  - The form asks for the main problem, why it matters, and the specific needs the project will address.
- **Methodology** (`Méthodologie`) — **1000 characters** shown.
  - The form asks for research steps, experimental techniques/tools/resources, and possible collaborations with institutions or partners.
- **Keywords** (`Mots clés`) — **80 characters** shown.
  - Keywords are entered separated by commas.
- **Expected results** (`Résultats attendus`) — **1000 characters** shown.
  - The form explicitly gives examples of tangible outputs: reports, scientific publications, databases, software, or other products.
  - It asks how deliverables contribute to knowledge advancement and practical application.
- **Beneficiary user sectors** (`Secteurs utilisateurs bénéficiaires`) — **200 characters** shown.
- **Expected scientific impact** (`Impact scientifique attendu`) — **500 characters** shown.
- **Expected socio-economic impact** (`Impact socio-économique attendu`) — **500 characters** shown.
- **Solutions to propose to decision-makers** (`Solutions à proposer aux décideurs`) — **500 characters** shown.
  - The form asks for concrete solutions for political and institutional decision-makers.

### Stage 3 — Planning des tâches
Observed structure:
- Task name (`Nom de la tâche`)
- Completion date (`Date d'achèvement`)
- Ability to add multiple tasks.

### Stage 4 — Potentiel scientifique et technique
The visible form contains a **project leader / carrier** section and then research-program participation information.

Observed project-leader fields:
- Full name (`Nom & prénoms`)
- Grade (`Grade`)
- Specialty (`Spécialité`)
- Status (`Statut`)
- Affiliated institution (`Établissement de rattachement`)
- Institution name if needed
- Laboratory code
- Laboratory title
- Agency
- Professional address
- Mobile phone
- Email
- Latest degree obtained
- Year obtained

The form also asks for the **three latest participations in research and development programs**, including national, international, multisectoral, etc. For each entry it shows:
- project/program title;
- year;
- organization.

The form provides an **Add member** action, indicating that team members can be added through the interface.

### Stage 5 — Scientific and logistical equipment available
Two separate sections are visible:

**Scientific equipment available**
- designation;
- location;
- functionalities;
- observations.

**Logistical equipment available**
- designation;
- location;
- functionalities;
- observations.

The interface allows multiple equipment entries in both sections.

### Stage 6 — Needs and estimation
Observed table fields:
- item type (`Type d'article`)
- item designation (`Désignation d'article`)
- item quantity (`Nombre d'article`)
- financial estimate in DZD (`Estimation financière (DA)`)

The form calculates and displays a total estimated amount.

### Stage 7 — Dissemination
Observed field:
- **Plan de dissémination des résultats** — strategies for dissemination and valorization of research results.
